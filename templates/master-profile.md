# Master profile

> This file is where you list everything you have done. The app builds every CV,
> cover letter and application answer from it, and it will not write anything
> that is not in here.
>
> You can edit it in the app (**Profile** in the header) or in any text editor.
> The app watches the file, so an edit in an editor shows up on your next click.
>
> ## The format
>
> A `##` heading opens a section, and everything under it is that kind of record.
> The sections are **Education, Projects, Findings, Roles, Publications, Skills,
> Awards** and **Service** — use the ones you need and delete the rest.
>
> A `###` heading starts one record, written `Title — Organisation` (that dash is
> an em dash). Under it come a few lines of detail, then a blank line, then what
> you actually did:
>
> ```
> ### Research Assistant — Some University
> id: role-some-university
> dates: 2023-01 .. 2024-06
> profiles: research, teaching
> tags: fieldwork, r, statistics
> location: Melbourne, Australia
>
> Ran the field season for a 40-site study, and rewrote the analysis pipeline in
> R so the whole thing reruns in one command.
> ```
>
> - **id** — a short unique name. Generated documents refer back to it, so do not
>   reuse one.
> - **dates** — `YYYY-MM .. YYYY-MM`. Leave the end blank for something ongoing
>   (`2023-01 ..`). A bare year works too.
> - **profiles** — which kinds of job this record is useful for. These labels are
>   yours; you start with `research, industry, teaching, public-sector` and can
>   change them in Settings.
> - **tags** — any keywords you like. Used to match records against a job ad.
> - **location** — optional.
>
> ## Two things worth knowing
>
> **Put your real numbers in.** If a generated bullet contains a figure, that
> figure has to appear in one of the records the bullet came from, or the app
> flags it. So write "reduced processing time from 6 hours to 20 minutes" rather
> than "reduced processing time substantially".
>
> **Write publications as normal citations.** The app reads them out of this file
> and formats them in APA itself, so a year or a journal name cannot get altered
> along the way.
>
