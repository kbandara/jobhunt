## Findings

### Flow regulation halves invertebrate turnover
id: finding-turnover
dates: 2024
profiles: research
tags: ecology, bayesian, community-assembly

Across 40 sites, regulated reaches showed 52% lower species turnover between
seasons than unregulated ones. The effect held after accounting for water
temperature and substrate, which had been the standing alternative explanation.

### Environmental flow releases work, but slowly
id: finding-releases
dates: 2025
profiles: research, public-sector
tags: environmental-flows, monitoring, policy

Community composition at released sites took 3 years to become distinguishable
from control sites — long enough that a standard 12-month monitoring contract
would have reported no effect.
