# Profile: public-sector — government, agencies and NGOs

The reader is likely a panel, working to a written set of criteria, and at least
one of them is not from the technical area. Applications are scored against the
criteria rather than read as a story, so every essential requirement in the
advertisement must be visibly answered somewhere in the document.

Section order: Summary, Experience, Technical Skills, Education, Service. Around three
pages — a guide, not a limit. Panels read long documents, and a criterion
answered too briefly scores as unanswered.

Lead with delivery in an organisational setting: what was owned, who relied on
it, what was reported to whom, and what changed. Stakeholder work, quality
assurance, reporting lines and handover are evidence here, not filler.

Register: plain. Expand acronyms on first use. Name the method in words a panel
member from another branch can follow, then the term if the ad uses it.

Publications are omitted entirely unless the advertisement asks for them.

If the advertisement carries selection criteria, they are answered in their own
document — do not fold criteria answers into the CV.

Where the candidate holds a security clearance and the profile-meta records one,
mention it once, accurately, and never upgrade its level or currency.

Call the technical section "Technical Skills".
