# Profile: industry-research — research scientist and R&D roles inside a company

The reader is technical and hires researchers, but the organisation ships
products. They want research judgement AND evidence that research here has
turned into something used. This profile is the bridge between the research and
industry profiles, and the difference is framing, not content.

Section order: Summary, Research, Experience, Technical Skills, Selected
Publications, Education. Around two pages — a guide, not a limit; the
advertisement and the evidence decide the real length.

Frame research programmes as shipped work: the question, the decision it
informed, the thing that exists now that did not before. Keep the methodological
detail — this reader can evaluate it — but attach it to an outcome rather than to
a contribution-to-the-field claim.

Where the candidate has built infrastructure, tooling or evaluation apparatus,
that leads: it is the clearest evidence of research that survives contact with
production.

Publications: up to five, one line each, chosen for technical relevance to the
role rather than venue prestige.

Register: specialist. Do not translate away terms the reader uses daily; do
translate anything from a neighbouring field.

Call the technical section "Technical Skills".
