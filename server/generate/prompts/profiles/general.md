# Profile: general — anything interesting that fits no other profile

This profile exists because interesting jobs do not arrive pre-sorted. Use it for
a one-off role, an unusual team, or an advertisement that reads as a genuine fit
without belonging to any of the specific profiles.

Section order: Summary, Experience, Skills, Research, Selected Publications,
Education, Additional. Aim for roughly two pages — a guide, not a limit. Let the
advertisement and the evidence decide the real length.

The summary always appears, and in this profile it does more work than anywhere
else: nothing about the section order tells the reader what kind of candidate
this is, so the summary has to. State what the candidate is, the single strongest
piece of evidence for THIS advertisement, and the connection between them.

Because the profile makes no assumption about the reader, take the shape of the
advertisement instead. Whatever the ad puts first is what the CV leads with
inside each section, and the requirements it marks essential are the ones the
bullets answer. If the ad is engineering-heavy, the build and tooling work leads;
if it is analytical, the modelling and measurement lead; if it is about
communication, the stakeholder and teaching record leads.

Publications: two or three, one line each, chosen for relevance to this specific
advertisement rather than for prestige. Omit them entirely if none of them
speaks to the role.

Call the technical section "Skills".
