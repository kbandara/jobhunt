Answer the application questions listed under "The questions" below.

One entry in `answers` per question, in the order given. Copy each question into
`criterion` exactly as it was written — assessors frequently score against its
literal wording, and a paraphrase costs marks before a word of the answer is
read. Never merge two questions, never split one, and never answer a question
that was not asked.

`paragraphs` are the paragraphs of the answer, one object per paragraph.

## How to answer

Use the STAR shape without labelling it: the situation and your task, the
specific actions you took, and the result. Lead with the action and the result —
an assessor reading forty responses is looking for what you did, not scene
setting.

Write in the first person and about your own contribution. Where the work was
collaborative, say what your part was rather than claiming the whole. "I built
the coding handbook for a five-person review team" beats both "we built" and an
implied solo effort.

One concrete example, told properly, beats three gestured at. Choose the single
strongest piece of evidence for each question and give it enough detail that an
assessor could picture it.

Mirror the language of the question itself. If it asks about "communicating with
influence", use that framing rather than a synonym.

Every paragraph must cite the evidence records it draws on in `evidence_ids`,
exactly as for a CV. Do not build more than two answers on the same evidence
record unless there is genuinely nothing else — an assessor notices when every
answer is the same story, and it makes a broad record look narrow.

## Length

Each question below carries either a word limit or "no stated limit".

Where a limit is given, aim for 90-100% of it. Coming in far under reads as
thin; going over risks the portal truncating the answer mid-sentence, which is
worse than being brief. The app counts the words of what you write and shows them
the count against the limit, so an answer that overshoots will be visible — write
to the limit rather than hoping.

Where no limit is given, write 250-350 words.

## Honesty

If the records do not support what a question asks for, do not manufacture an
example. Answer with the closest genuine experience, let the gap show, and add
an entry to `gaps` naming what was missing. An invented example is the fastest
way to fail the interview that probes it — these questions are exactly what an
interviewer reads back to you.
