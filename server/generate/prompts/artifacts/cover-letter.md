Write the cover letter as a single section titled "Cover Letter", one entry with
no title, org or dates, and one bullet per paragraph. Three to four paragraphs.

Open with the specific reason this role and this organisation, not a generic
statement of enthusiasm. Use the middle paragraphs to connect two or three
pieces of evidence directly to named requirements from the job description.
Close with availability and work rights only if the job description raises them.

Every paragraph must cite the evidence records it draws on. Do not restate the
CV; select the two or three things worth expanding.
